Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

DashboardTerms of Service

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXTPAGE and the related Social Watch dashboard (“Service”) handles information. It describes the Service as currently implemented. Features that are not yet enabled are not described as active data collection.

1. Information handled

  • Sign-in and session data: the dashboard checks a configured username and password, and uses a session cookie to keep an authorized session active. The configured password is not stored as a user profile in the dashboard database.
  • Dashboard content: alert messages, sources, keywords, priorities, timestamps, settings, integration status, and operational logs may be stored in the database.
  • Uploaded library files: files you upload to the NEXTPAGE library, along with titles, file names, categories, notes, sizes, and timestamps. The library is designed for private, authenticated access.
  • AI feature data: prompts and AI conversation history may be stored. Files attached to an AI chat are processed to provide the requested response and may be sent to the AI gateway configured by the Service operator.
  • Technical data: hosting providers may process IP address, browser/device information, request timestamps, and security or error logs as part of operating and protecting the Service.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate access; operate the dashboard; store and retrieve alerts, settings, and uploaded files; provide AI features requested by an authorized user; troubleshoot errors; protect the Service; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Storage and service providers

The Service currently uses Railway for application hosting, PostgreSQL for structured records, and Cloudflare R2 for private file storage when configured. AI requests may be routed to the AI gateway and model provider configured by the operator. Discord and Home Assistant may receive alert or automation data when those integrations are configured. These providers process information to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Social-network integrations

At the effective date of this Policy, TikTok and Instagram OAuth connections and social publishing/monitoring are not implemented in this Service. The Service does not currently request or store TikTok or Instagram passwords or access tokens. If social integrations are added later, this Policy will be updated to describe the data requested, purposes, retention, and deletion choices before those features are made available.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the infrastructure and integration providers listed above, disclosed when required by law, or shared when needed to protect the Service, its users, or others. Alert content may be sent to configured Discord or Home Assistant integrations according to the dashboard settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is kept for as long as needed to operate the Service, maintain security and records, or meet legal obligations. Authorized dashboard users can delete supported alerts and library assets and clear AI conversation history using the available controls. Backups, security logs, and provider-side records may persist for a limited period under the applicable provider's retention practices.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the Service, including authenticated access and private storage configuration. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your credentials confidential and notify the administrator if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact the administrator who provided access so the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

9. Your choices and requests

You may use available dashboard controls to delete certain content. For access, correction, or deletion requests involving other records, contact the administrator who provided your account or access. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal, or operational reasons.

10. Changes and contact

This Policy may be updated as the Service changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. For privacy questions or requests, contact the administrator who provided your access. A public privacy contact email has not yet been designated.
